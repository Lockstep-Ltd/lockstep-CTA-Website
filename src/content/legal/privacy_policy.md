Lockstep (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal data responsibly and transparently. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you concerning our practices. Lockstep is a UK-based digital platform designed to help people manage their gambling behaviour through practical tools, community access, and real-world alternatives. It is not a gambling service, advertising platform, or therapeutic provider.

## Agreement & Consent

By accessing or using the Lockstep website, mobile application, or any related platform or service (together, the “Service”), you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection, use, and handling of information in accordance with it. This applies regardless of how Lockstep is accessed, including via mobile devices, web browsers, or future platforms. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should not access or use the Service.

## Data we collect

Lockstep collects only the information necessary to operate the service and to understand how it is used.

### Account and Identification Data

If you choose to create an account or contact us, we may collect basic identifying information such as your name and email address. Where authentication or analytics tools assign unique identifiers (for example, device or session identifiers), these may be collected to support functionality and security. If, in future, Lockstep offers sign-in via third-party providers (such as Apple or similar services), we may receive limited information from those providers, typically a name and email address, in accordance with your settings on the third-party platform.

### Usage and Interaction Data

We may collect limited information about how the platform is used, such as interactions with tools, features, or daily activities logged by the user. This information is used to operate the service and to improve relevance and usability over time. Lockstep does not collect usage data for advertising or behavioural profiling.

### Communications and Feedback

If you contact us directly with questions, feedback, or support requests, we may collect the information you choose to provide. Participation in surveys, questionnaires, or research requests is entirely optional, and responses are used only to understand user needs and improve the service.

### Social Media Interactions

Lockstep may maintain a presence on social media platforms such as Instagram, LinkedIn, or similar services. If you choose to interact with Lockstep through these platforms, we may receive personal information that you elect to share publicly or directly with us. The companies that operate these social media platforms may also provide us with aggregated or anonymised analytics relating to engagement with our pages. Lockstep does not control how third-party social media platforms collect or process data.

### Cookies & Analytics

Lockstep uses only cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary for the operation and security of the service. These essential technologies enable core functionality and are not used for advertising, marketing, or cross-site tracking. Lockstep may use privacy-focused analytics tools to understand how the website or platform is used in aggregate. This information helps us improve usability, performance, and reliability. Where analytics are used, they are configured to avoid identifying individual users. They do not rely on cookies, do not track users across websites or sessions, and do not collect personal data such as names, email addresses, or IP addresses. All analytics data is processed in a way that prevents individual identification and is used solely for internal analysis.

For more information about cookies, please visit <https://allaboutcookies.org/> .

## How we use information

Lockstep uses information in a limited and intentional way to operate the service, improve its usefulness, and ensure that facets of the service remain relevant to each user. We process personal data on the basis of user consent, performance of the service, and legitimate interests where appropriate. Information provided by users, such as stated interests, preferences, and interactions with tools, may be used to tailor how content, real-world alternatives, and available meetings are ordered and presented within the Service. This may include the use of relevance scores to surface meetings that are more likely to be more suitable, based on shared characteristics or interests. This personalisation is designed to improve relevance and usability within Lockstep and is not used for advertising, cross-site tracking, or third-party profiling. Lockstep may also use information in aggregated and anonymised form to understand how the platform is used overall, to assess the effectiveness of features, and to guide future improvements. Aggregated data does not identify individual users and may be shared at a high level with partners or stakeholders, for example as general usage statistics.

If you contact Lockstep directly, information you provide may be used to respond to enquiries, provide support, or communicate important service-related updates. Where contact details have been provided, we may also send occasional non-promotional updates about Lockstep, such as changes to the service or early access availability. You can opt out of non-essential communications at any time. Where outbound links are shown within the Service, some may be affiliate links. These do not affect the price paid by the user. Recommendations are prioritised based on relevance and suitability rather than commercial relationships. Lockstep does not sell personal data and does not share personal data with advertisers for behavioural or cross-site targeting.

## Affiliate & outbound links

Lockstep may include outbound links to third-party products, services, experiences, or resources that are intended to support healthier, real-world alternatives to gambling. Some of these outbound links may be affiliate links, meaning Lockstep may receive a commission if a user chooses to follow the link and completes an action, at no additional cost to the user. Affiliate relationships do not influence how recommendations are prioritised or presented. Alternatives and suggestions are ranked based on relevance, suitability, and alignment with user interests and context, rather than commercial arrangements. Lockstep does not sell personal data and does not share personal data with advertisers or affiliate partners for behavioural advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking. Any interaction with a third-party website or service is governed by that third party’s own terms and privacy practices.

Retention periods vary depending on the type of data and the purpose for which it is used. ## Lockstep Chrome Extension

*Last updated: 27th August, 2026*

This section describes the data practices of the Lockstep: Gambling Content Blocker Chrome extension, available on the Chrome Web Store.

### What the extension does

Lockstep blocks gambling-related content on YouTube, Google Search, Ecosia, Instagram, and known gambling websites, using on-device keyword and domain matching.

### Data we collect

The extension stores two small values locally, using Chrome's built-in storage API:

- A count of how many items have been blocked
- Your display preference (whether blocked content is replaced with a placeholder card or hidden completely)

These values are synced only through your own Google account across your own Chrome installations (via Chrome's native sync), the same way your browser bookmarks or settings sync. They are never sent to Lockstep's servers or any third party - Lockstep does not operate a backend server for this extension, and the extension makes no network requests of any kind.

### What we don't collect

Lockstep does not collect, transmit, or sell any of the following:

- Your browsing history or page content
- Personally identifiable information
- Analytics or usage tracking data
- Any data to third parties or advertisers

### Why the extension requests broad site access

Lockstep requests permission to read and modify pages on YouTube, Google Search, Ecosia, Instagram, and a list of known gambling domains. This access is used exclusively to scan page content locally in your browser for gambling-related material and block or replace it. No content from these pages is read, stored, or transmitted anywhere outside your device.

### Data Retention & Feedback

The two stored values (blocked count and display preference) remain on your device (and in your Chrome sync, if enabled) until you reset the counter in the extension popup or uninstall the extension, at which point Chrome removes the stored data automatically.
