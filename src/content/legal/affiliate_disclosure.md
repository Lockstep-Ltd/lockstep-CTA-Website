Lockstep may include links to third-party products, services, experiences, or organisations that are intended to support healthier, real-world alternatives to gambling. Some of these outbound links may be affiliate links, meaning Lockstep may receive a commission if a user chooses to follow the link and complete an action, at no additional cost to the user. Affiliate relationships are used to help support the development and operation of the platform. They do not influence how recommendations are prioritised or presented. Suggestions are surfaced based on relevance to the user, including stated interests and context, rather than commercial arrangements.

Lockstep is currently in the process of verifying and establishing affiliate relationships with third-party networks and partners. This may include participation in affiliate networks such as Awin, as well as direct partnerships with individual providers. Not all outbound links are affiliate links, and some may simply direct users to relevant external resources.

Third-party websites may display their own advertising or promotional content, including gambling-related advertisements. Such advertising does not reflect Lockstep’s values and is strongly discouraged. However, Lockstep does not control the content, marketing practices, or advertising placements on third-party websites once a user has chosen to leave the Service.

Lockstep does not accept paid placements that compromise user trust, does not sell user data, and does not share personal data with affiliate partners for advertising or tracking purposes. Affiliate partners do not receive access to personal data beyond what is required to complete a transaction initiated by the user on the partner’s platform.

This disclosure will be updated as affiliate relationships evolve.
