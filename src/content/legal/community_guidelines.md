Lockstep exists to help people manage their relationship with gambling in a healthier and more positive manner.

For many, gambling is not just a financial issue. It brings isolation, secrecy, and a gradual detachment from other people. Over time, this can make it harder to speak openly, ask for help, or feel understood. Lockstep is designed to address that directly. The platform combines practical tools with structured community features to support what is widely recognised as positive behavioural change in a more grounded and consistent way. Users can take part in small, focused group meetings, share posts and experiences, and form connections with others on a similar path.

Lockstep can be used anonymously. There is no requirement to share your real identity. This is intentional. Gambling often carries stigma and shame, and many people find it difficult to open up, even to themselves. Anonymity lowers that barrier and allows people to engage honestly, without fear of judgement or exposure. At the same time, we encourage users to be open where they feel able to. Gambling-related problems are not something to be ashamed of. The industry is highly sophisticated and heavily marketed, with significant resources invested in influencing behaviour at scale. As a result, more people are affected than is often recognised. Acknowledging the problem, whether privately or openly, is a step forward.

Connections on Lockstep are mutual. Both people must choose to connect. This avoids follower counts, comparison, and the pressure that often comes with traditional social platforms, and instead encourages more meaningful, two-way relationships. These community features are not designed for passive use. They exist to reduce isolation, create accountability, and allow progress to be seen and shared in a constructive way.

Alongside this, Lockstep places a strong emphasis on helping users understand gambling clearly and realistically. This includes the mathematical realities behind odds and expected loss, the behavioural patterns that develop over time, and the wider impact gambling can have, including isolation, dishonesty, and loss of control.

The aim is not to overwhelm, but to provide clarity. Better decisions come from seeing things as they are.

Everything on Lockstep - the tools, the content, and the community - is built around one outcome: helping users move forward, consistently, and with greater awareness.

## Respect Each Other

Lockstep is a shared space. How you behave here directly affects other people using the platform. Users are expected to treat others with respect. This means communicating without hostility, avoiding personal attacks, and recognising that others may be in a vulnerable position.

Harassment of any kind is not allowed. This includes repeated unwanted contact, targeting individuals, or attempting to undermine or intimidate other users. Threats are not tolerated, whether direct, implied, or suggestive. This includes threats towards individuals or groups.

Users must respect the privacy of others. Do not share, request, or attempt to expose personal information about another user. This includes trying to identify someone outside the platform or sharing any information that could compromise their anonymity. Respect also includes boundaries. If someone does not want to engage, that should be accepted. Connections on Lockstep are mutual, and users should not attempt to bypass or pressure others into interaction.

Hate speech or discriminatory behaviour is not allowed. This includes any content that attacks or degrades others based on characteristics such as race, gender, sexuality, religion, or background. In that same vein, content or behaviour that encourages harm is not permitted. This includes promoting or normalising destructive behaviour, whether directed at yourself or others.

Lockstep is specifically designed to move people away from harmful gambling behaviour. As such, users must not promote, encourage, or normalise excessive gambling. This includes discussing wins in a way that encourages others to participate, promoting gambling platforms or services (whether sponsored or not), or encouraging gambling beyond someone’s means.

Users should also avoid creating pressure or comparison. Lockstep is not a competitive environment. Progress will look different for different people, and interactions should reflect that. In meetings, these expectations apply equally. Do not interrupt, dominate discussions, or disrupt the session. Everyone should be able to participate without feeling uncomfortable or pressured. If you feel uncomfortable interacting with another user, you can use in-app tools such as blocking to manage your experience without needing to submit a report.

Ultimately, users are responsible for how they show up here. The expectation is simple: act in a way that supports your own progress without negatively affecting someone else’s.

## Respect Lockstep

Lockstep is built to be a safe, focused environment. Using the platform in a way that undermines that purpose is not allowed.

Users must not use Lockstep for spam or mass messaging. This includes sending repeated unsolicited messages, posting the same content across multiple areas, or attempting to drive traffic or attention for personal gain.

Accounts must represent real individuals. You may remain anonymous, but you must not impersonate another person, create misleading identities, or present yourself in a deceptive way.

Attempts to manipulate the platform are not permitted. This includes artificially influencing engagement, creating multiple accounts to bypass restrictions, or using tools, automation, or scripts to interact with the platform in a non-genuine way.

Users must not attempt to bypass moderation or enforcement actions. This includes creating new accounts after being suspended or banned, or using alternative accounts to continue activity that has already been restricted.

The reporting system must be used responsibly. Submitting false, misleading, or abusive reports in an attempt to manipulate outcomes or target other users is not allowed.

Lockstep must not be used to promote or facilitate scams, financial exploitation, or dishonest activity. This includes any attempt to mislead users for personal gain.

Users must not attempt to interfere with the platform itself. This includes trying to access, disrupt, or damage accounts, systems, or data, or using deceptive methods to obtain sensitive information from others.

All use of Lockstep must comply with applicable laws and regulations. This includes respecting intellectual property, avoiding illegal activity, and not using the platform to facilitate behaviour that could cause harm to others.

We expect users to use Lockstep in good faith. If behaviour consistently undermines the platform, even if it is not explicitly listed here, we may take action to protect the integrity of the community.

Lockstep will continue to evolve. As new risks or behaviours emerge, we may update these guidelines and take action where necessary to maintain a safe and effective environment.

## Moderation & Enforcement

Lockstep uses a combination of user reporting and manual review to maintain a safe and focused environment.

Users can report posts, users, and interactions, including behaviour within meetings. Reports are reviewed by a member of the Lockstep team. We do not use automated systems to make final decisions. Each report is assessed based on the content, the context, and any previous behaviour associated with the account. Not all reports will result in action. Where appropriate, reports may be dismissed if no clear violation has occurred. Where a breach is identified, action will be taken in line with the guidelines.

Lockstep applies a progressive approach to enforcement. In less serious cases, a warning may be issued. Warnings are recorded against an account and are used to identify repeated behaviour. Continued violations after a warning may result in stronger action.

Where necessary, content may be removed. This typically applies to posts or interactions that do not meet the standards outlined in these guidelines. In more serious or repeated cases, access to parts of the platform may be restricted. This can include temporary suspension from posting, connecting with other users, or participating in meetings. Suspension periods may vary depending on the severity of the behaviour. For severe or persistent violations, accounts may be permanently banned. A banned account will lose access to Lockstep and may not create or use additional accounts to bypass this restriction.

Moderators may record internal notes on user accounts. These notes are used to provide context around previous reports, behaviour, or decisions, and may be considered when reviewing future reports. These notes are not visible to other users.

Attempts to avoid enforcement actions, including creating new accounts after suspension or ban, will result in further action.

We prioritise safety and fairness in all moderation decisions. This means that action may be taken without prior warning where behaviour presents a risk to other users or to the integrity of the platform.

If you believe a moderation decision has been made in error, you can contact us at [moderation@lockstep.uk](mailto:moderation@lockstep.uk). We will review the case, but we are not obligated to reverse decisions.

In order to ensure a frictionless experience users also have access to tools such as blocking, which allow them to limit or prevent interaction with other users without initiating a formal report. Blocking is a private action and does not automatically trigger moderation review.

Lockstep’s moderation approach will continue to evolve as the platform develops. We may take action in situations that are not explicitly outlined in these guidelines where it is necessary to protect users and maintain a safe environment.
